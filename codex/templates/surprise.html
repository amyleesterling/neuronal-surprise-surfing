How the surprises are picked

Every cell is scored against its peer group on two signals: total partner count (z-scored vs. same-cell-type peers), and how rare its input/output neuropil pair is across the whole connectome. We surface only the most extreme outliers on each signal — usually fewer than 30 cells out of 139,255.
